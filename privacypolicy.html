MyCal AI Privacy Policy

MyCal AI stores tracker information on your device. You can export a JSON backup and choose where to save or transfer that file. Backups are not uploaded by the app.

Health Connect

With your permission, MyCal AI reads step-count records from Health Connect to show daily steps and estimate walking calories. It does not write health records or read other health categories. You can revoke access in Health Connect settings. Daily step totals are included in JSON backups you choose to export.

Meal analysis

If you use AI meal analysis, the image or meal description you submit is sent to Google's Gemini API using the API key you provide. Do not include information you do not want to send to that service.

Control and contact

Tracker data stays on your device unless you export a JSON backup and choose to share or store it elsewhere. JSON backups do not include your AI key or Health Connect permission. Clearing the app's data removes its local copy.
