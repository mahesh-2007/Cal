About

Cal is a Cal AI–style tracker rebuilt as a single HTML file. Interface follows a near-black canvas, Inter with tabular numerals, and three macro accents. Nutrition values are estimates, not medical measurements — research on photo-based trackers found they can underestimate calories, especially from fats.
